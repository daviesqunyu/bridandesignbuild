PROJECT DESCRIPTION

Grand Daddy Restaurant, Bungoma

  • Category: Hospitality / Restaurant Development
  • Location: Kibabii–Manyanya Road, Bungoma
  • Status: Ongoing – 2026
  • Scope: Design and Build Services

Project Overview

Grand Daddy Restaurant is a contemporary hospitality development designed as a complete dining and entertainment destination. Its distinctive architectural identity is defined by a dramatic wave-form roof that creates generous, naturally ventilated dining spaces while giving the restaurant a memorable presence along Kibabii–Manyanya Road.

The development brings together expansive indoor dining, executive booths, a nyama choma zone, a commercial kitchen, landscaped gardens and private outdoor gazebos within one coordinated hospitality environment.

What We Are Delivering

  • Architectural concept development and detailed space planning.
  • Design of the restaurant’s distinctive curved roof structure.
  • Planning of indoor dining, executive booths and the nyama choma zone.
  • Design and coordination of the commercial kitchen and service areas.
  • Integration of landscaped gardens, outdoor seating and private gazebos.
  • Coordination of architectural, structural and building-services requirements.
  • Construction management through Bridan’s integrated design-and-build approach.

Key Design Features

  • A dramatic wave-form roof that establishes a strong visual identity.
  • Curved steel trusses creating spacious, unobstructed dining areas.
  • Naturally ventilated interiors designed for guest comfort.
  • Executive booths and multiple dining zones for different customer experiences.
  • Landscaped gardens and private gazebos for outdoor dining.
  • Charcoal-coloured roofing complemented by warm timber soffits.
  • Stone-clad columns that provide texture, durability and visual character.
  • A purpose-planned commercial kitchen supporting efficient restaurant operations.

Client Benefits

  • A distinctive hospitality destination with strong roadside visibility.
  • Flexible indoor and outdoor dining options for individuals, families and groups.
  • Improved guest comfort through spacious planning and natural ventilation.
  • Efficient separation of dining, food preparation and service functions.
  • A coordinated design-and-build process that reduces delivery and coordination gaps.
  • A memorable architectural identity that strengthens the Grand Daddy brand.

Project Statement

Grand Daddy Restaurant demonstrates Bridan Design Build’s ability to combine expressive architecture, efficient hospitality planning and integrated project delivery. The result is a welcoming dining destination designed to become a recognisable social and culinary landmark in Bungoma.

About us

Bridan Design Build Ltd. is a locally owned company incorporated in Kenya. We offer a straightforward method of project delivery.
